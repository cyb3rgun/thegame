## State at the close of G04

G03 met its goal in its first piece: Lvl_RailTest plays a 207 m route end to end with two held encounter beats, cover and aiming through the one screen space path (D-019, D-020). The season went on to graphics settings with six measured presets and a benchmark (D-025 to D-031), the first visual pass with night lighting and mannequin bodies (D-033 to D-035), and the main menu with level selection and a pause menu (D-036 to D-038).

G04 met its goal over four pieces:

- **G04-B01.** Style scoring with combos, controlled pairs, head shots, ranks and run statistics; ammunition, reload and weapon switching, with cover as the reload on the rail; the hostage taker on the door range and as a rail beat; hit stop, camera kick and the kill screen effect as measured data; Overclock time dilation (D-043 to D-046).
- **G04-B02.** Brand colours, the logo crosshair and the holographic HUD; per bone hit zones with reactions and the disarm; the cyberpunk grade (D-047 to D-051, D-056, D-057).
- **G04-B03.** Weapons as data on one weapon actor, the PCP pressure model, and the 3R 9x19, AP JET I and AP JET II with placeholder bodies; respawn in encounter levels; the pistol confirmed as the disarm target (D-052 to D-055, D-058, D-059).
- **G04-B04.** The repository moved to cyb3rgun/thegame with the old repository kept as a backup, the README became the project front page, and the documentation was reconciled with what is built (D-060 to D-062).

Carried out of G04, open in the backlog: the MetaHuman cast (D-039 to D-042 reserved), real weapon models on the mount point sockets, environment art beyond blockout, the night benchmark run, and every scenario beyond the door range, rail and zombie test levels.

The next season has no goal yet.
